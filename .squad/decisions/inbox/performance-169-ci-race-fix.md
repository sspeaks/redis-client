### 2026-09-15: PR 169 CI race fix for bounded multiplexer backpressure
**By:** Performance
**What:** Reordered multiplexer slot completion so admission is released before a waiter is signalled, moved writer ownership handoff to after a successful send, and added a one-shot writer top-up pass before flushing an underfilled batch.
**Why:** The MVar waiter-queue rewrite widened two pre-existing ordering windows enough for CI scheduling to observe them. First, callers could wake on a completed response before admission bookkeeping had released their slot, so `muxStatsOutstanding` could still read non-zero and a blocked submitter could stay asleep a little longer. Second, the writer could publish slots to the reader before their bytes were written and could also flush an underfilled first batch before same-burst submissions had a chance to join it, producing nondeterministic `[2,2]` or `[1,2,2]` batch logs instead of the intended greedy `[2,2,1]` drain. The new ordering makes completion visibility follow committed admission state, keeps in-flight batches writer-owned until send succeeds, and gives a just-woken producer burst one scheduler yield to fill the current batch without violating the configured limit.
